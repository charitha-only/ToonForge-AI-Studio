# Character bible

## `CHR-ELIO-01` — Elio

| Attribute | Locked specification |
| --- | --- |
| Species / silhouette | Young red fox; compact pear-shaped torso, large triangular ears, one oversized tapered tail with cream tip. |
| Proportions | Head is about 40% of body-plus-head; short legs; tail reaches ground when standing; never adult/tall proportions. |
| Face / eyes | Rounded muzzle, charcoal button nose, espresso oval eyes with two small amber catchlights; ear angle carries expression. |
| Palette / material | Copper fur `#B85F36`; cream `#F4D6A8`; inner ears `#D88A72`; paws/nose `#332A2B`; matte soft short fur. |
| Outfit | Deep teal woven neckerchief `#1E5961`, one brass star clasp `#C8903D`; no other clothing. |
| Behavior / movement | Curious then considerate; ears forward while listening, tail curls around paws when worried; three light steps then head tilt. |
| Voice | Young, warm, quiet, lightly breathy original direction; not an actor imitation. |
| Permanent constraints | Four limbs, one tail, one neckerchief/clasp; no stripes, extra clothing, blue fur, or changed tail tip. |

## `CHR-GLIM-01` — Glim

| Attribute | Locked specification |
| --- | --- |
| Species / silhouette | Original palm-sized floating teardrop light creature, exactly three petal-like luminous fins, no limbs. |
| Face / palette | Two midnight-blue dot eyes; amber core `#FFD36A`, white-gold center `#FFF1B5`, mint edge `#9BE0C6`. |
| Material | Soft volumetric light, transparent edges, faint dust trail; never hard glass. |
| Behavior / movement | Skittish while enclosed, inquisitive when free; three-beat pulse before direction change; one clockwise orbit after release. |
| Voice | No spoken language; bell-like chirps are planned sound design only. |
| Permanent constraints | Never larger than Elio’s head; no wings, arms, humanoid form, harsh beam, or protected-character resemblance. |

## Relationship and scale lock

Glim remains above/beside Elio’s eye line except for its intentional nose touch in `SH-030-010`. Elio starts screen left facing right through `SC-010` and `SC-020`; the final wide shot preserves their forward rightward travel.
