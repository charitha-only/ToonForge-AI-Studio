# Story synopsis

## Logline

In a moonlit magical forest, a curious young fox discovers a tiny light creature trapped in an abandoned lantern and learns that patience—not force—can turn a rescue into a friendship.

## Emotional objective

Elio shifts from curious urgency to gentle attentiveness. Glim’s choice to trust him is rewarded by a forest-wide visual reveal. The opening hook is a lantern pulsing beneath blue moss; the payoff is a living star field around two new friends.
