## Mix handoff

Keep dialogue, ambience, music, and effects on separate tracks. Preserve room tone through every cut, duck music only as needed for dialogue intelligibility, then recheck captions against final voice. Delivery loudness, stems, and mastering targets require the user’s actual platform/editor specification; none are assumed here.
