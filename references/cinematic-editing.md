# Cinematic editing and finishing

## Editorial map

Build an edit decision list from approved shots rather than render order. Each edit records outgoing shot, incoming shot, cut/transition intent, continuity justification, audio bridge, and status. A transition must either preserve time/place/action or explicitly communicate the change.

## Continuity at the cut

Check screen direction, eyelines, looking room, hand/prop position, lighting direction, costume condition, and sound perspective on both sides of every cut. Use a bridge, insert, cutaway, or revised prior shot when a hard cut makes a state change look accidental.

## Rhythm and coverage

Cut on an action, reaction, information reveal, or deliberate musical/emotional turn—not merely because a generated clip ends. Hold an establishing frame long enough to orient the audience, then vary framing only when it improves clarity or feeling. Do not manufacture exact timing claims without access to an actual edit/timeline tool.

## Picture lock and delivery

Mark picture lock only when shot order, visual states, and edit transitions are approved. Finalize captions, mix, color/visual consistency, titles/credits, and delivery export after picture lock. If any picture change occurs, mark affected audio/QC records for recheck.
