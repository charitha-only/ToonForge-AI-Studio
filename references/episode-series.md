# Episode and series development

## Series bible (minimum viable)

- **Premise and promise:** recurring emotional/comedic/adventure engine.
- **Audience and format:** platform, episode range, aspect ratio, dialogue density, accessibility needs.
- **Visual rules:** approved style frames, palette/lighting, motion grammar, prohibited drift.
- **World rules:** locations, technology/magic limits, recurring props and their logic.
- **Cast:** character packet IDs, relationships, wants, fears, voice/performance notes, scale chart.
- **Continuity rules:** what persists, reset rules, chronology, wardrobe/location states.
- **Episode engine:** setup → complication → escalation → payoff → new status quo.
- **Asset library:** reusable environments, props, FX, audio motifs, and approval/version owner.

## Episode packet

Record episode ID/title/logline, change from prior episode, beat sheet, scene list, dialogue/audio plan, asset needs, shot ledger, and delivery spec. Start with a duration-aware beat estimate, but do not claim precise runtimes without the user’s editing tool and pace.

## Scene-to-shot handoff

For each scene, map story beat → visual beat → shot ID → start/end state → source references → prompt → acceptance status. An episode is not ready to edit until every transition has an owner and all required shots have an approved or clearly flagged fallback.
